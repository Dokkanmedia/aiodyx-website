From paper files to one digital file per employee

Before

Employee data sits in paper files and scattered sheets, and leave is requested by email. Expiring iqamas and contracts are found late, and Qiwa data does not match company records.

After

Each employee has one file with contract, documents, leave, appraisals and custody. Requests are approved from the phone, alerts arrive before every expiry, and contract data matches Qiwa.

End to end, step by step

  1. 1Recruit

    The job is posted, and applicants move through screening and interviews to the offer.

  2. 2Hire and contract

    The accepted applicant becomes an employee with a contract holding wage, occupation and term, documented in Qiwa.

  3. 3Onboard

    The onboarding checklist runs: documents, custody, accounts and GOSI or SSC registration.

  4. 4Manage day to day

    Leave, requests and appraisals run through self-service, and alerts track document expiry.

  5. 5Offboard

    On resignation or termination, custody is returned, end-of-service dues are calculated and the file is closed.

Time off and absences

Leave types and balances follow labour law and your policy: annual, sick, Hajj, marriage and maternity. Balances accrue monthly and stay visible to the employee.

Common questions

Can we migrate from Jisr, Bayzat or Excel?

Yes. We move employees, contracts, leave balances and documents, and check balances with your team before go-live.

Does it calculate end-of-service benefits?

Yes. We configure it to Saudi or Jordanian labour law and your policy, linked to the final settlement in payroll.

Who can see salaries and personal data?

Only the people you authorise. Managers see their team's job data, while salaries and sensitive documents stay with HR and finance.

Does it connect to GOSI and Social Security?

Yes. We prepare registration and contributory wage data for GOSI in Saudi Arabia and SSC in Jordan, with contributions calculated in payroll.

Do employees need training for self-service?

Very little. The interface is in Arabic and simple, and we provide a short guide and an intro session for managers and staff.
