From copying orders by hand to a store linked to the warehouse

Before

Someone copies Salla or Zid orders into the system every morning. Store stock does not match the warehouse, so out-of-stock items sell and orders get cancelled. Waybills are typed by hand on the courier's site.

After

The order lands in the system the moment it is paid, and stock and prices update on the store automatically. The warehouse picks the order, the waybill is created from it and the customer gets the tracking number on WhatsApp.

End to end, step by step

  1. 1Receive the order

    The order arrives from the Odoo store, Salla, Zid or Shopify straight into the system.

  2. 2Confirm payment

    Payment by mada, Apple Pay or card is recorded and matched to the order.

  3. 3Pick and pack

    A delivery order appears for the warehouse and stock is reserved and deducted.

  4. 4Ship and track

    The waybill is created and the tracking number reaches the customer by email or WhatsApp.

  5. 5Invoice and reconcile

    The invoice is issued and reported, and gateway payouts are matched to orders in the bank account.

Common questions

We already run a Salla store. Do we need to move to Odoo?

No. Your Salla store stays as it is and we connect it so orders flow in and stock and prices update automatically. The same applies to Zid and Shopify.

When should we choose a store on Odoo?

When you need special prices for wholesale customers, a B2B portal, or tighter links to stock and accounting without another subscription.

How often does stock update on the store?

On an Odoo store it is instant. With Salla, Zid and Shopify, sync runs every few minutes or on each stock move.

Are online orders reported to FATOORA?

Yes. Each paid order becomes a simplified or standard tax invoice depending on the customer and is reported to FATOORA, or to JoFotara for Jordanian companies.

How long does the integration or store build take?

Connecting an existing store we estimate at two to three weeks. A new Odoo store takes four to six weeks depending on catalogue size and design.
