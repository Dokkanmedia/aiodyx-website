From a vehicle spreadsheet to a live record per vehicle

Before

Vehicles live in a spreadsheet nobody updates. Fuel and workshop bills get lost between accounting and operations. A registration expires and the vehicle is stopped, and a fine arrives with no idea who was driving.

After

Each vehicle has a file with its driver, contract, fuel and service. Alerts arrive before anything expires. Management sees monthly cost per vehicle with no manual collation.

End to end, step by step

  1. 1Register the vehicle

    Plate, chassis, model, registration and insurance dates, and purchase or lease value.

  2. 2Assign the driver

    The vehicle is handed to an employee as custody with a date and odometer reading, keeping past drivers on file.

  3. 3Log usage

    Odometer readings, fuel fill-ups and fines are logged against the vehicle as they happen.

  4. 4Service and renew

    Alerts for service and contract expiry, with each service logged with its cost and workshop bill.

  5. 5Analyse cost

    A monthly report of cost per vehicle, driver and category, to decide whether to keep or replace.

Every detail

Vehicle register

Each vehicle has a full file: plate, chassis, model, year and fuel type. Vehicles are grouped by status, category and site. Registration and insurance copies are attached.

  • Plate, chassis and model details
  • Statuses like active or in workshop
  • Categories like cars, trucks, equipment
  • Registration and insurance attachments

Drivers and custody

Each vehicle is linked to the employee who drives it, with handover and return dates. The next driver can be scheduled in advance. The driver history stays on every vehicle.

  • Vehicle linked to the employee file
  • Handover date and odometer reading
  • Schedule the next driver
  • Full history of past drivers

Fuel and odometer

Each fill-up is logged with litres, amount and odometer. The system works out consumption and flags odd figures. Drivers can submit fuel receipts from their phone as expenses.

  • Fill-up log with litres and amount
  • Dated odometer readings
  • Compare consumption across vehicles
  • Fuel receipts from the phone

Scheduled service

Each service is logged with its type, cost and workshop. Oil changes, tyres and periodic inspection come up as reminders. The workshop bill in accounting is linked to the vehicle.

  • Service types with their cost
  • Periodic inspection reminders
  • Workshop bills linked to the vehicle
  • Breakdown and accident history

Contracts, insurance and fines

Lease and insurance contracts are logged with start, end and recurring cost. The system alerts ahead of expiry by a set lead time. Traffic fines are logged on the vehicle and assigned to that day's driver.

  • Leases with a monthly instalment
  • Insurance and registration expiry alerts
  • Fines assigned to the responsible driver
  • Deduct fines from payroll if needed

Cost reports

Fuel, service, lease and insurance roll up into one cost per vehicle. Costs can be charged to a cost centre or project. Management knows what each vehicle and branch costs.

  • Monthly cost per vehicle
  • Analysis by category and branch
  • Charge to cost centres
  • Keep-or-replace decisions

Common questions

Does the system track vehicle location live?

The Fleet app manages records, cost and contracts. Live location needs an external tracking device, and linking its data can be scoped based on your provider.

How are traffic fines recorded?

The fine is logged on the vehicle with date and amount, and the driver is taken from that day's custody record. It can become a payroll deduction if your policy says so.

Does it work for heavy equipment too?

Yes. You can define categories for trucks, forklifts and equipment, and track hours or odometer depending on the asset.

Are fuel and workshop bills linked to accounting?

Yes. A vendor bill line is linked to the vehicle, so the cost shows on its file and in financial reports with no double entry.

Can we import vehicles from Excel?

Yes. We import vehicles, drivers and expiry dates from your current sheet and review the data with you before go-live.
