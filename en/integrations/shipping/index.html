Waybills created from the delivery order

Carriers such as Aramex and SMSA handle deliveries within Saudi Arabia, Jordan and abroad. AIODYX connects your carrier account to Odoo Inventory so waybills and labels are created straight from the delivery order.

Book a free consultationPackages

Why this integration matters

Copying the customer's address and parcel weight into the carrier's website wastes warehouse time and causes address errors. Cash-on-delivery amounts get tracked in separate spreadsheets and settle late.

What happens automatically

  • Waybill created when the delivery order is validated
  • Shipping label printed from Odoo
  • Tracking number saved on the order and sent to the customer
  • Cash-on-delivery amount passed to the carrier
  • COD remittances matched to invoices

How we connect it

  1. 1Create the waybill from the delivery order
  2. 2Print the label
  3. 3Track the shipment
  4. 4Cash-on-delivery reconciliation

What we need from you

  • A business account with the carrier and its API credentials
  • Warehouse addresses and product weights in Odoo
  • A label printer or standard printer, depending on volume

Frequently asked questions

Can we use more than one carrier?

Yes. The carrier is chosen per order, manually or by a rule based on city or weight.

Does shipping cost show on the quote?

You can add a shipping line at a fixed price or based on rules you set on the order.

How does the customer track the parcel?

They receive the tracking number by email or WhatsApp, and it also appears in the Odoo customer portal.

Does it work with online store orders?

Yes. Orders from Salla, Zid, Shopify and the Odoo store go through the same delivery order and ship the same way.
