What you actually gain from Point of Sale?

✓Fast checkout

Scan, pay with mada and print the receipt in a few taps on a touch screen.

✓Every receipt reported electronically

Every simplified invoice carries a QR code and is reported to FATOORA or JoFotara, depending on the country.

✓Faster daily till closing

The system computes the expected amount per payment method; the cashier only enters the count.

✓Live stock deduction at sale

Each sale deducts from the branch warehouse, so you see real stock across branches.

From a standalone till to a connected checkout

Before

The till runs on a separate device and sales are keyed into accounting at day end. Stock never matches, cash differences surface late, and e-invoicing needs yet another system.

After

The cashier sells from a tablet, the receipt is reported automatically and stock is deducted at once. At closing, differences show per payment method and the journal entry is created without manual work.

End to end, step by step

  1. 1Open the session

    The cashier logs in and enters the opening cash balance.

  2. 2Add items

    Scan a barcode, weigh on the scale or tap the item; promotions apply automatically.

  3. 3Take payment

    Cash, mada, Apple Pay, or split across several methods.

  4. 4Issue the receipt

    The simplified invoice prints with a QR code and is reported to the e-invoicing platform.

  5. 5Close the session

    Count the till, review differences and post the entry to accounting automatically.

Common questions

What happens if the internet goes down?

Selling continues and receipts still print. When the connection returns, sales sync to the system and invoices are reported to the platform.

Do we need new hardware?

Usually not. It runs on any tablet or PC with a modern browser, and we check your existing printers and scales before starting.

Can we connect our existing mada terminals?

It depends on the terminal provider. We review your terminal and bank or gateway and suggest a direct link or the best alternative.

Is it compliant with FATOORA phase 2?

Yes. Odoo supports phase 2 simplified invoices, and we onboard your devices with the platform and test reporting before go-live.

How long does POS go-live take?

For one branch, we estimate two to three weeks including products, prices, hardware and cashier training. Extra branches go faster.
