From paper archives to every document in its place

Before

Contracts sit in a cabinet, with copies in someone's inbox or laptop. Getting a contract signed means printing, scanning and a long wait. An iqama or commercial registration expires and nobody notices until too late.

After

Each document is uploaded once and linked to its record. A contract goes out as a signing link and comes back signed and filed automatically. The system alerts the owner before any document expires.

End to end, step by step

  1. 1Upload the document

    Drag and drop, scan, or email it to the folder's address.

  2. 2Tag and link

    It goes into its folder with tags and is linked to a customer, employee or project.

  3. 3Prepare for signing

    Place signature, date and name fields, and set signer roles and order.

  4. 4Send and sign

    The link arrives by email, can be shared on WhatsApp, and each party signs on the phone.

  5. 5File and follow up

    The signed copy and its certificate are filed on the record, and expiry tracking starts.

E-signature

Upload the contract, place the signature fields, and send it to one or more parties in a set order. Signers open the link and draw or type their signature on any device. It is a simple e-signature suited to commercial contracts and internal forms.

Expiry alerts

We add expiry dates to key documents: commercial registration, municipal licences, iqamas and passports. The system creates a reminder for the owner ahead of a lead time you choose. One report shows everything expiring this month.

Common questions

Is the e-signature legally binding?

It is a simple e-signature with a log of signers and times, commonly used for commercial contracts and internal forms. For transactions that require a government-certified digital signature, check with your legal adviser.

Does the signer need an account?

No. Signers receive a link and sign in the browser on any device without registering.

Can we migrate our current archive?

Yes. We move files into the new folder structure and link what we can to customers and employees, depending on how well current files are named.

How do iqama and CR expiry alerts work?

We add an expiry date to the document or employee file and set a rule that creates a task for the owner a chosen number of days ahead.

Can the signing link go out on WhatsApp?

The link goes by email by default and can be copied and shared. With WhatsApp Business connected, we send it from the system using an approved message template.
