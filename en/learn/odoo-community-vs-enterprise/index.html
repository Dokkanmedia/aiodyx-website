Odoo Community or Enterprise? How to choose

How the two editions differ in apps, hosting, licensing and upgrades, when the free edition is enough, and when you need Enterprise.

The 30-second summary
  1. Community is free and open source; Enterprise adds apps and services under a per-user licence.
  2. Apps such as Payroll, Helpdesk, Sign and Documents are Enterprise-only.
  3. Decide by the apps you actually need, hosting, and support and upgrades.

Odoo comes in two editions. Community is open source and free under the LGPL licence. Enterprise is a paid subscription to Odoo S.A. that adds apps and features on top of Community. Both share the same core, so the real question is whether your business needs the extra apps and features.

The list of apps in each edition changes between versions. Check the official comparison page on odoo.com for the version you plan to use before deciding.

01

Apps available only in Enterprise

These are the main apps not available in Community in recent versions:

  • Full Accounting: Community provides invoicing and basic entries. Financial reports such as the balance sheet, profit and loss, the tax report, advanced bank reconciliation and asset management are in Enterprise.
  • Payroll and Appraisals.
  • Helpdesk and Field Service.
  • Sign, Documents and Knowledge.
  • Odoo Studio for changing screens and fields without code.
  • Quality and PLM for manufacturing.
  • The Barcode app for warehouses, Planning, Subscriptions and Rental.
  • Spreadsheet editing and advanced dashboards.

Many apps exist in both editions, such as Sales, Purchase, Inventory, basic Manufacturing, Point of Sale, CRM, Project, Employees, Time Off, Attendances and Website. Enterprise sometimes adds features to them, such as forecasting, scheduling or links to other Enterprise apps. The mobile experience is also more complete in Enterprise.

02

Hosting options

  • Odoo Online: cloud hosting managed by Odoo. Enterprise only. Quick to start, but it does not allow custom code modules. Customisation is done through Studio.
  • Odoo.sh: Odoo's cloud platform for Enterprise. It allows custom modules and provides staging branches, backups and a GitHub link. It suits companies that need custom development.
  • On-premise: on your own servers or a cloud of your choice. Available for both editions. You get full control, but you carry maintenance, backups, security and upgrades.
03

Licensing, upgrades and support

An Enterprise subscription is priced mainly per user and paid to Odoo S.A., directly or through a partner. Plans differ in the hosting and customisation they allow, so check the current terms on odoo.com. The subscription includes the upgrade service to new versions and Odoo's support for bugs in the standard product. In Community, upgrades are your responsibility and usually need technical effort to migrate data between versions.

04

When Community is enough

  • You need sales, purchase, inventory and invoicing without advanced financial reports inside the system.
  • You have a technical team to run the server, backups and upgrades.
  • You do not need Payroll, Helpdesk or Sign inside Odoo.
  • You accept using third-party modules and owning their quality and maintenance.

Watch the cost of filling gaps. Building financial reports or payroll on Community needs development and ongoing maintenance with every version. In many cases that cost exceeds the Enterprise subscription.

05

When you need Enterprise

  • You want full accounting with ready financial and tax reports.
  • You want payroll, attendance and leave in one system.
  • You need a helpdesk, e-signatures or document management.
  • You prefer managed hosting and guaranteed upgrades without an in-house technical team.
06

Conclusion

Start with the list of processes you want to run, map them to the apps in each edition, then add up hosting, maintenance and upgrade costs over several years. As an official Odoo partner, AIODYX helps companies in Amman and Riyadh make this comparison based on their actual processes.

FAQ

Can we move from Community to Enterprise later?

Yes. Enterprise sits on top of Community, and the same data carries over. The move needs planning and testing, especially with custom modules.

Can we install custom modules on Odoo Online?

No. Odoo Online does not accept custom code modules. If you need custom development, choose Odoo.sh or on-premise.

Who receives the Enterprise licence fee?

Odoo S.A., based on users and plan. The implementation partner's fees for setup and training are separate from the licence.
