Common questions

Do we need an official WhatsApp Business account?

Yes. Bulk sending requires a verified WhatsApp Business API account and a dedicated number. We help with registration and template approval from Meta.

Is there a cost per WhatsApp message?

Meta charges per conversation depending on its type and country. We explain the expected cost up front based on your campaign volume.

Does the editor support right-to-left text?

Yes. Emails and templates support Arabic and right-to-left layout, and we prepare templates in your brand style.

Can we bring in customers from Salla or Zid?

Yes. Once the store is connected, orders and customer data flow in automatically and become part of your segments and campaigns.

How are unsubscribe requests handled?

Every email has an unsubscribe link, and anyone who opts out joins a blacklist and gets no further campaigns. The same applies to WhatsApp opt-outs.
