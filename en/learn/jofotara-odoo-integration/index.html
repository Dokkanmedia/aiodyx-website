Connecting Odoo to Jordan's JoFotara e-invoicing system

What JoFotara is, how to get the client ID and secret from the taxpayer portal, how Odoo submits invoices and receives the QR code, and how credit notes reference the original invoice.

The 30-second summary
  1. JoFotara is Jordan's national e-invoicing system, run by the Income and Sales Tax Department.
  2. The connection needs the client ID and secret from your company's portal account.
  3. A credit note must reference its original, registered invoice.

JoFotara is Jordan's national e-invoicing system, run by the Income and Sales Tax Department (ISTD). It receives invoices from taxpayers' systems, records them and returns a QR code for each invoice as proof of registration. Taxpayers can enter invoices manually on the portal or connect their accounting system to send them automatically. This guide covers the second option with Odoo.

Scope, obligations and any deadlines are set by ISTD and may change. Check the current requirement that applies to your business with the Income and Sales Tax Department.

01

Why integrate instead of entering invoices by hand

Manual entry means typing every invoice twice: once in your system and once on the portal. That doubles the time and invites errors in amounts, taxes and buyer data. The invoice you hand to the customer also needs the QR code issued by the national system. With integration, the invoice leaves Odoo already registered and carrying its code, with no extra step for the accountant.

02

Getting the client ID and secret key

  1. Log in to your taxpayer account on the JoFotara portal.
  2. Open the system integration section and create new integration credentials for your system.
  3. Copy the client ID and secret key and store them somewhere secure.
  4. Note the income source sequence number shown in your account. The setup needs it.

These credentials amount to the right to issue invoices in your company's name. Do not share them by email or chat. Enter them directly in Odoo settings. Your implementation partner does not need your portal password.

03

What Odoo's l10n_jo_edi provides

Odoo includes the Jordanian e-invoicing localisation. Once it is active, you enter the client ID, secret key and income source sequence number in the accounting settings, and set your taxpayer type. That type follows your registration: income tax only, general sales tax, or special sales tax.

  • Building the invoice in the XML format the national system expects.
  • Sending the invoice to JoFotara from the invoice screen.
  • Receiving the QR code and printing it on the invoice.
  • Showing the send status and error messages on each invoice.
  • Sending credit notes with a reference to the original invoice.
04

Credit notes and returns

Once an invoice is registered in JoFotara, it cannot be edited. Any return, later discount or correction goes through a credit note. The note must reference the original invoice, and that original must already be registered in the system. In Odoo, you create the credit note from the original invoice, so the link is kept automatically. Write the return reason clearly because it is part of the data sent.

05

Points that need attention

  • Taxpayer type: a type that does not match your registration causes rejections.
  • Buyer data: check when the buyer's tax number or national ID is required, and align customer records with it.
  • Taxes: make sure every tax in Odoo maps to the right code, especially exempt lines or lines under special tax.
  • Rounding: the Jordanian dinar uses three decimal places, so review rounding on amounts and taxes.
  • Point of Sale: decide how POS invoices will be sent and test it before go-live.
06

Pre-go-live checklist

  1. An active taxpayer account on the JoFotara portal.
  2. Client ID, secret key and income source sequence number.
  3. Tax number and company name matching the ISTD registration.
  4. Your taxpayer type and the invoice types you issue.
  5. A review of taxes in Odoo and of customer data.
  6. Tests for a cash invoice, a credit invoice, a credit note and a POS invoice.
  7. Training accountants to follow up unsent invoices and error messages.
07

Conclusion

Connecting Odoo to JoFotara removes double entry and ensures every invoice leaves with its code. The technical part is ready in Odoo. The real work is setting up the taxpayer type, taxes and customer data correctly. FAQ

Do we need to share our portal password with the partner?

No. Your team generates the client ID and secret key and enters them in Odoo.

What if an invoice fails to send?

The error message appears on the invoice in Odoo. You fix the data and resend from the same screen.

Can a registered invoice be cancelled?

It cannot be edited or deleted. You reverse it fully or partly with a credit note that references it.
