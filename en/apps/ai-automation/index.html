Every detail

Automatic document reading

Email or upload a vendor bill and the system extracts vendor, date, amounts and VAT. The bill appears as a draft ready to review and post.

  • Vendor bills and expense receipts
  • Works with photos and PDFs
  • Learns from your corrections
  • Dedicated inbox for bills

Data validation

Before posting, the system checks the bill. It flags duplicates, missing VAT numbers and differences from the purchase order.

  • Duplicate bill detection
  • Match against PO and receipt
  • VAT number check
  • Low-confidence items flagged for review

Workflow rules

Set a condition and an action, and the system does the rest. For example, a PO above a limit goes to the finance manager, and an expiring contract creates an HR task.

  • Multi-level approvals
  • Tasks and follow-ups created automatically
  • Status changes and assignment
  • Changes without custom code

Smart alerts

The system watches what matters and tells you before it becomes a problem. Alerts arrive in the system, by email or on WhatsApp.

  • Customer over credit limit
  • Iqama or contract about to expire
  • Item running low
  • Overdue invoice

Arabic chat assistant

Ask the way you speak: how much did we sell this month in the Jeddah branch? The assistant answers from your system data, shows the records it used and stays within the user's permissions.

  • Questions on sales, receivables and stock
  • Respects each user's access rights
  • Shows the source with the answer
  • Summarises long records and threads

Predictive insights

Using past sales, the system estimates expected demand and suggests purchase quantities. These are estimates that support decisions and do not replace them.

  • Demand forecast per item
  • Suggested reorder quantities
  • Expected customer collections
  • Unusual figures flagged

Common questions

Where does our data go when we use AI?

Before we start, we show you which service is used and what data is sent to it. The assistant reads only what the user's account allows, and you can limit it to specific departments.

How accurate is reading Arabic invoices?

Accuracy is high on clear printed invoices and lower on poor photos or handwriting. That is why every bill stays a draft until someone reviews it.

Can the system post an entry without approval?

You decide. Clear recurring cases can post automatically while the rest wait for review.

Do we need a technical team to change the rules?

Most workflow rules are edited in settings without code. We train someone on your team to do it, in Arabic.

Does the assistant answer questions outside company data?

We configure it to answer from your system data and approved documents. If it cannot find the answer, it says so.
