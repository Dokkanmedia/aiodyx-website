FATOORA Phase 2 with Odoo: a practical guide

What clearance and reporting mean, how to onboard an e-invoicing unit with an OTP, what CSIDs and the cryptographic stamp do, the most common rejection reasons, and a pre-go-live checklist.

The 30-second summary
  1. B2B invoices are cleared before they reach the customer; simplified B2C invoices are reported after issue.
  2. The e-invoicing unit is onboarded with an OTP from the FATOORA portal, then the system receives a signing certificate.
  3. Odoo ships a Saudi localisation; the real work is setup, testing and cleaning customer data.

FATOORA is the e-invoicing platform of Saudi Arabia's Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority (ZATCA). Phase 1 required invoices to be generated electronically in a set format. Phase 2, the integration phase, requires your invoicing system to connect to the platform and send every invoice to it. This guide explains what actually happens when you run the integration from Odoo and what you need to prepare first.

ZATCA notifies businesses of their Phase 2 date in waves. We do not list dates or revenue thresholds here because they change. Check your own date and current requirements directly with ZATCA or on the FATOORA portal.

01

Clearance and reporting: two invoice flows

Phase 2 separates the standard tax invoice from the simplified tax invoice. Each type follows a different path with the platform.

  • Clearance: applies to standard B2B invoices. The system sends the invoice to FATOORA, the platform validates it and returns it stamped. Only then do you share it with the customer.
  • Reporting: applies to simplified B2C invoices, such as point-of-sale receipts. The customer gets the invoice immediately, and the system reports it to the platform within 24 hours.
  • Credit and debit notes follow the type of the original invoice. They must reference it and state the reason for issuing.
02

Onboarding the e-invoicing unit with an OTP

Every system that issues invoices is registered with ZATCA as an e-invoicing generation solution (EGS) unit. In Odoo, each sales journal is onboarded as its own unit. If you have several branches or journals, each one needs its own onboarding.

  1. Complete the company record in Odoo: VAT number, a company identifier such as the CR number, and the full national address.
  2. Log in to the FATOORA portal with the business account and generate an OTP to onboard a new unit. The code expires quickly, so have Odoo ready before you generate it.
  3. Enter the code in the sales journal's ZATCA settings in Odoo. The system creates a certificate signing request (CSR) and sends it to ZATCA.
  4. ZATCA returns a compliance CSID. Odoo uses it to submit test invoices covering the types you will issue.
  5. Once the compliance checks pass, Odoo requests the production CSID. Real invoices are sent with it.

Odoo supports separate environments: a developer sandbox, a simulation environment and production. Always start with simulation on a test copy of your database. Move to production only after tests pass on real data.

03

The cryptographic stamp and QR code

Every Phase 2 invoice is produced as XML following the UBL 2.1 standard. The system signs it with the cryptographic stamp using the unit's certificate. The QR code carries the key invoice data with the signature, so any party can verify it. Each invoice is also chained to the previous one through a hash value and a sequential counter. That is why a sent invoice cannot be deleted or edited. Corrections go through a credit or debit note.

04

What Odoo's l10n_sa_edi does

The Saudi e-invoicing localisation is part of Odoo. You do not need external middleware. The module handles the following:

  • Onboarding journals as e-invoicing units and obtaining compliance and production CSIDs.
  • Building and signing the XML for each invoice and generating the QR code.
  • Sending standard invoices for clearance and reporting simplified ones.
  • Showing the invoice status and any warning or rejection messages on the invoice itself.
  • Connecting Point of Sale to the same flow for simplified invoices.

Odoo usually decides the invoice type from the customer type. A customer set up as a company gets a standard invoice, and an individual gets a simplified one. That makes clean customer records an essential step.

05

Common rejection reasons

  • An invalid buyer VAT number, or one typed with spaces or Arabic-Indic digits.
  • An incomplete buyer address on a standard invoice: building number, district, city or postal code.
  • A business customer set up as an individual, so a simplified invoice goes out instead of a standard one, or the reverse.
  • A credit note with no reference to the original invoice or no reason.
  • A wrong tax code, such as an exempt or zero-rated line with no exemption reason.
  • Missing seller data, especially the national address and the company identifier.

The platform separates warnings from rejections. An invoice with a warning is accepted, but fix the cause early before it becomes a rejection in a later update.

06

Pre-go-live checklist

  1. Confirm an active FATOORA portal account with rights to generate OTPs.
  2. Review company data: VAT number, CR number and national address.
  3. List the journals, branches and points of sale that need a unit.
  4. Clean customer data: customer type, VAT number and address.
  5. Review taxes and exemption reasons on products and services.
  6. Test each type: standard, simplified, credit note, debit note and POS receipt.
  7. Train the accounting team to read rejection messages and resend.
  8. Name one person to check unsent invoices every day.
07

Conclusion

The technical connection in Odoo is ready. Most problems come from data. Focus on clean customer and tax data, and test in simulation before production. The AIODYX team in Riyadh and Amman handles this setup, onboarding and testing, and can review your data readiness before your date.

FAQ

Do we need a unit for every branch?

Each sales journal is onboarded as its own unit in Odoo. The number of units depends on how you split journals, branches and points of sale.

Can we edit an invoice after clearance?

No. You correct it with a credit note that references the original, then issue a new invoice if needed.

What if the connection to the platform drops?

The invoice stays in Odoo as unsent, and you can resend it when the connection returns. Watch simplified invoices closely because they have a reporting window.
